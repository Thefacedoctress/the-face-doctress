Policies & Important Information

Late Policy

Please communicate any anticipated lateness. Clients who are more than 10 minutes late will be subject to a $10 late fee. Clients who are more than 20 minutes late may have their appointment canceled or may be subject to a $20 late fee.

Same-Day Appointments / Rescheduled Appointments

Same-day appointments must be booked at least 6 hours prior to the appointment time. If booking an appointment less than 6 hours before the scheduled start time, please text or call 910-445-1813 to confirm availability.

Rescheduled appointments must also be rescheduled at least 6 hours prior to the scheduled start time.

After-Hours Appointments

Clients wishing to book an appointment outside of regular business hours may text or call 910-445-1813 to inquire about availability.
