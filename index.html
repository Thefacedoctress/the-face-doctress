Booking Policies

Deposits

A $15 deposit is due upon booking. Deposits are non-refundable.

Sickness Policy

Please reschedule your appointment if you are feeling sick. Appointments may be rescheduled if signs of sickness are present.

Skin Conditions

Please reschedule your appointment if you have open wounds, blisters, or sores in areas being serviced.

Payments

The remaining balance is due at the time of your service. We currently accept Cash, Cash App, Zelle, or payment through Square.

Late Policy

I do not charge a late fee. However, clients more than 5 minutes late may need to reschedule their appointment. Please communicate any anticipated lateness.

Refund Policy

All sales are final. No refunds.

Guests

Only the person receiving service may enter. No extra guests allowed.

Restrooms
