Booking Policies

Please review these policies before scheduling your appointment.

01

Deposits

A $15 deposit is due upon booking. Deposits are non-refundable.

02

Sickness Policy

Please reschedule your appointment if you are feeling sick. Appointments may be rescheduled if signs of sickness are present.

03

Skin Conditions

Please reschedule your appointment if you have open wounds, blisters, or sores in areas being serviced.

04

Payments

The remaining balance is due at the time of your service. We currently accept Cash, Cash App, Zelle, or payment through Square.

05

Late Policy

Please communicate any anticipated lateness. Clients who are more than 10 minutes late will be subject to a $10 late fee.

Clients who are more than 20 minutes late may have their appointment canceled or may be subject to a $20 late fee.

06

Same-Day & Rescheduled Appointments

Same-day appointments must be booked at least 6 hours prior to the appointment time.

If booking less than 6 hours before the scheduled start time, please text or call 910-445-1813 to confirm availability.

07

Refund Policy

All sales are final. No refunds.

08

Guests

Only the person receiving service may enter. No extra guests allowed.

09

Restrooms

No public restrooms available.
