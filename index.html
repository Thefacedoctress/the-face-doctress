Booking Policies

Deposits

A $15 deposit is due upon booking. Deposits are non-refundable.

Sickness Policy

Please reschedule your appointment if you are feeling sick. Appointments may be rescheduled if signs of sickness are present.

Skin Conditions

Please reschedule your appointment if you have open wounds, blisters, or sores in areas being serviced.

Payments

The remaining balance is due at the time of your service. We currently accept Cash, Cash App, Zelle, or payment through Square.

Late Policy

Please communicate any anticipated lateness. Clients who are more than 10 minutes late will be subject to a $10 late fee. Clients who are more than 20 minutes late may have their appointment canceled or may be subject to a $20 late fee.

Same-Day Appointments / Rescheduled Appointments

Same-day appointments must be booked at least 6 hours prior to the appointment time.

If booking an appointment less than 6 hours before the scheduled start time, please text or call 910-445-1813 to confirm availability.

Rescheduled appointments must also be rescheduled at least 6 hours prior to the scheduled start time.

After-Hours Appointments

Clients wishing to book an appointment outside of regular business hours may text or call 910-445-1813 to inquire about availability.

Refund Policy

Guests
